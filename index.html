How SSS is estimated5% × ₱35,000 maximum Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) = ₱1,750 employee share. The current SSS schedule uses a 15% total contribution rate, split between employee and employer. The ₱35,000 MSC cap applies from 2025.
PhilHealth employee contribution₱1,200⌄
How PhilHealth is estimated₱48,000 × 5% total premium rate ÷ 2 = ₱1,200 employee share, assuming ₱48,000 is the applicable monthly basic salary and no other adjustment applies.

Automatic holiday integration

Set your normal workdays once. Holiday premiums are estimated automatically for each saved month when a holiday falls on a scheduled workday.

Holiday premiumsAutomatic per monthIncluded in the corresponding 15th or month-end cutoff calculation.
Multipliers usedRegular holiday: 200% for first 8 hours; 260% for overtime beyond 8 hours. Regular holiday on rest day: 260% first 8 hours; 338% for overtime. Special non-working day: 130% first 8 hours; 169% for overtime. Special non-working day on rest day: 150% first 8 hours; 195% for overtime. Night differential adds 10% of the applicable hourly rate for hours from 10 PM to 6 AM. Special working days have no statutory holiday premium. For monthly-paid employees, this calculator adds the premium portion on top of the ordinary monthly salary, not the ordinary day's base pay again.

Holiday pay is estimated only when the automatic holiday-work assumption is enabled and a holiday falls on one of your selected scheduled workdays. Actual attendance, rest-day status, special proclamations and company payroll rules can change the result.

Unpaid absences are recorded separately inside each cutoff's expanded details. Paid leave should not be entered as an unpaid absence.
